The problem

What CBSE is
getting wrong

01

Opaque marking

No visibility into how sheets are evaluated or why marks were deducted. Students are left guessing.

02

Unaffordable re-evaluation

Re-checking fees are high enough to be a barrier. Your right to appeal shouldn't depend on your wallet.

03

Dangerously late results

Re-evaluation results arrive after college admission deadlines, destroying futures without warning.

04

Zero accountability

Examiners who make errors face no consequences. There is no independent redressal system.

Our demands

What we're
asking for

1

Free answer sheet photocopies for every student who requests them — no questions asked.

2

Lower re-evaluation fees to a nominal amount. Full waiver for students from BPL families.

3

Results within 15 days of applying — well before any college admission deadline.

4

An independent appeals committee with actual student representation, not just CBSE officials.

5

Examiner accountability — documented penalties for verified marking errors.
